More music.

Jazz Watch gathers verified free jazz livestreams into a simple calendar, so finding the next set takes a little less work.

It is a companion idea to Classical Watch, with a focus on jazz clubs, music schools, and performance series. This is selective coverage, not a complete guide to every jazz concert.

A small, independent project

Jazz Watch is presented by Stringfest Analytics. The venues and performers produce the broadcasts and retain their rights. Listing an event does not imply a partnership or endorsement.

Support the project by sharing it or introducing someone who could use Stringfest’s Excel and analytics work.

Privacy

This version has no visitor accounts, analytics tags, advertising, third-party fonts, embedded video, or tracking cookies. Filters stay in the page URL so you can bookmark or share the view. Your browser requests the calendar data from the site’s host.

When you open a venue or other external link, that service’s privacy and cookie policies apply. The hosting provider may retain normal server logs.

Corrections and suggestions

Visit Stringfest Analytics to get in touch. Include the official event or livestream link, the date, and evidence that the video is free to watch. Suggestions are reviewed before publication.
